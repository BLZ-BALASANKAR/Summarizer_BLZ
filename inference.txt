GEMINI ANALYSIS
================================================================================

Metric interpretations:
Compression %: Text reduction; lower is more concise.
Density %: Info per word; higher is richer.
Cosine Similarity: Semantic match to original; higher is better.
Time (s): Generation speed; lower is faster.

Performance rankings:
Extractive: Sumy Textrank (0.9425) leads quality. Custom Textrank/Sumy Lexrank (0.031s) are fastest.
Abstractive: Flan T5 Base (0.924) leads quality. T5 (11.512s) is fastest.
Best speed: Custom Textrank, Sumy Lexrank.
Best quality: Sumy Textrank (extractive), Flan T5 Base (abstractive).
Best balance: Tfidf, Custom Textrank.

Red flags & issues:
Pegasus Xsum: Very low Cosine Sim (0.5229), low Density, extremely slow (97.039s). Indicates poor quality, potential hallucinations.
Semantic Textrank: Slow (5.977s) for extractive. Abstractive models are very slow.

Practical recommendations:
Speed-critical: Custom Textrank, Sumy Lexrank.
Quality-critical: Sumy Textrank (extractive), Flan T5 Base (abstractive, if speed allows).
Balanced: Tfidf, Custom Textrank.
Avoid: Pegasus Xsum.

Trade-offs:
Speed vs Quality: Faster extractive models offer good quality. Slower abstractive models can achieve higher quality but at significant time cost.
Extractive vs Abstractive: Extractive is faster, more faithful (use for faithfulness). Abstractive generates novel sentences, potentially higher compression/density, but slower and riskier (use for rephrasing).
